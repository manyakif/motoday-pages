# mo.Today Support

mo.Today is a time-management app that helps you remember what matters today.
If you have a question or run into a problem, please check the topics below.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I'm not receiving notifications
In iOS Settings > Notifications > mo.Today, check that notifications are allowed.
You can set a notification time when you create a to-do.

### My calendar is empty
In iOS Settings > mo.Today, check that Calendar permission is turned on.
You can also choose which calendars to display from the Settings screen inside mo.Today.

### Weather isn't showing
In iOS Settings > mo.Today, check that Location permission is set to "While Using the App."
Location is used only to retrieve today's weather and is not stored separately.
Please also check your internet connection.

### iCloud sync isn't working
In iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud, make sure you are signed in and that the mo.Today toggle is on.
Syncing happens automatically over a Wi-Fi or cellular connection.

### How do repeating to-dos work?
A repeating to-do automatically creates a new occurrence on the schedule you set — daily, weekly, monthly, and so on.
Each occurrence can be marked complete or incomplete independently.

### What is the difference between a to-do and a routine?
A to-do is a task that is finished once it is done, while a routine is a habit you repeat regularly.
Routines let you see how consistently you have kept them through statistics.

### How is my data backed up?
All of your mo.Today data is automatically backed up through iCloud.
When you sign in with the same Apple ID on another iOS device, your data syncs automatically.

## Tips

- Swipe a card left or right to move between dates.
- Press and hold a card to open the edit screen.
